# 📊 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

This project analyzes an Online Retail dataset (2010–2011) to understand sales performance, customer behavior, and product-level revenue trends.

## 🚀 Tools Used
- Power BI
- SQL

### 1) Sales Overview

- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Top Countries by Revenue
- Top Products by Revenue

### 2) Customer Insights & Segmentation
- Customer segmentation (High / Mid / Low value)
- Repeat vs One-Time Customers
- Orders vs Revenue scatter analysis
- Top 10 customers by revenue

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Revenue peaks in late 2011 indicating strong seasonal demand.
- United Kingdom contributes the majority of revenue.
- A small number of products generate a large share of revenue.
- High-value customers contribute disproportionately to total revenue.
